Professional Summary

Mr. Atluri serves as a Traffic Engineer with diverse experience in all facets of Urban Traffic Engineering and Transportation Planning. His expertise encompasses design of traffic signal systems, traffic signal timing, signing and pavement markings, and construction traffic control.

He has worked with numerous government agencies and has a thorough understanding of their rules and regulations — including TxDOT, City of Houston, Harris County, FDOT, SCDOT, and GDOT. His project experience spans traffic studies, impact analyses, master plans, transit, aviation, and intelligent transportation systems.
