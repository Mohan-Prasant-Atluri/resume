About Me

I serve as a Traffic Engineer with diverse experience in all facets of Urban Traffic Engineering and Transportation Planning. My expertise encompasses all aspects of design of traffic signal systems, traffic signal timing, signing and pavement markings, and construction traffic control.

I have worked with several government agencies and have a thorough understanding of their rules and regulations. My experience spans traffic studies, impact analyses, transportation master plans, transit and BRT, aviation landside planning, and intelligent transportation systems across Texas, Florida, Georgia, and South Carolina.
